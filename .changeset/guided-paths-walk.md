New `qa-path` skill (user-invoked, `/qa-path`). It writes the document a human walks the app with: ordered journeys through the interface, each with numbered steps in the screen's own words, the behaviour the reader should observe, and numbered questions they answer back by email or in Word. It covers a whole project (the discovery guide a client receives with a new version: what the version delivers with sourced numbers, the vocabulary kept from the old system, what changes and why, what comes later, the paths, which migrated records to compare, how to answer, and an appendix of every question), a branch (what the change alters, plus the screens around it), or one feature, and decides the scope from the invocation without asking. It writes from the requirements on the tracker and from the app as it is: every label is confirmed in the templates or translations before a step names it, every number comes from the import report, the database, or the spec, and anything unsourced is marked `À confirmer`. The Markdown lands in `qa/<slug>.md`, uncommitted, and a client-facing path is rendered through `witify-docx` in the `witify-writing-style` voice. One plugin eval covers it: pointed at a ticket with no tracker MCP server, it stops and points at the install step instead of inventing the requirements.
