New `figma-brief` skill (productivity): write a concise brief, a prompt, for generating interface mockups with an AI design tool such as Figma Make. It spends its words on what the tool cannot guess (the business purpose of each screen, the fr-CA vocabulary, one primary action per screen, the business rules that shape the UI, how the screens link for a clickable prototype) and none on pixels, states the global conventions once, gives labels rather than data rows, and delivers the whole brief in one code block ready to paste.
